# Course Creator Quality Protocol

```text
Design for a defined learner, prerequisite level and learning outcome.
Chunk long source materials into concept cards before synthesis.
Every lesson needs objective, explanation, activity, assessment and homework.
Assessments must align with objectives.
Do not turn source material into a passive summary-only course.
```

## Gates

```text
Learner fit < 8.5: revise.
Objective-assessment alignment < 9.0: revise.
Lesson usability < 8.5: revise.
Source coverage < 8.5: revise.
Activity quality < 8.0: revise.
```
