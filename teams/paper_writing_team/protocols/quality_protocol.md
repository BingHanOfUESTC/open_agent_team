# Paper Writing Quality Protocol

```text
Do not invent experiments, results, baselines, citations, limitations or reviewer concerns.
Long inputs must be chunked into evidence cards before synthesis.
Every claim about results must trace to a figure, table, log, note or Boss statement.
Related work must compare, not list.
The paper must make a clear contribution claim and state limitations.
Rebuttal responses must be respectful, evidence-backed and action-oriented.
```

## Gates

```text
Contribution clarity < 8.5: revise.
Evidence traceability < 9.0: revise.
Section coherence < 8.5: revise.
Citation safety < 9.0: revise.
Reviewer-response specificity < 8.5: revise.
```
