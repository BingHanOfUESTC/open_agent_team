# Business Plan Quality Protocol

```text
Do not invent market size, revenue, customer traction, partnerships or financials.
Separate facts, assumptions and strategic recommendations.
Use evidence cards for long reports and customer materials.
Make the business model, customer pain, differentiation and GTM explicit.
Financial plans must expose assumptions.
```

## Gates

```text
Customer/problem clarity < 8.5: revise.
Evidence traceability < 9.0: revise.
Business model coherence < 8.5: revise.
Financial assumption clarity < 9.0: revise.
Risk realism < 8.5: revise.
```
