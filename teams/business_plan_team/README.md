# Business Plan Team

`business_plan_team` turns an idea, product notes, market evidence and founder assumptions into a business plan and investor-ready narrative.

Use `@team_lead_agent` as the entry point.

## Long Input Handling

Research reports, customer notes, analytics exports and financial spreadsheets must be chunked into evidence/assumption cards before synthesis.

## Default Delivery

```text
delivery/business_plan.md
delivery/pitch_narrative.md
delivery/financial_assumptions.md
delivery/risk_register.md
delivery/source_trace.md
```
